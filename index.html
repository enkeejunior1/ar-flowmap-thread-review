When applied naively, FlowMap fails in two ways on AR video:

1. Drifting: errors build up across chunks over long rollouts
2. Low-quality motion: fast-moving regions turn blurry and distorted

Why do these two failures happen?

Yonghyun Park @handle · 4/13
Failure mode 1: Drifting.

In SF-DMD, a bidirectional teacher scores the whole video at once.

In AR FlowMap, the causal teacher conditions on the student's already-drifted history. Trained only on clean video, the teacher fails to fix the error and continues the drift.

How do we fix drifting without DMD?

We train a drift model online on student rollouts to imitate its drifted transitions, and use it as the negative branch of guidance.

Subtracting the drift direction pushes the teacher back toward clean video!

Failure mode 2: Low-quality motion.

Unlike DMD, a FlowMap student has to learn the teacher's velocity field across long steps.

Patches with larger motion have a much less smooth velocity field (higher Lipschitz), making fast motion hard to learn in a few steps.

Guidance controls this smoothness.

In chunk 1, CFG smooths the velocity field (weak CFG fails). From chunk 2 on, CFG stops helping since text does not guide frame-to-frame motion.

Skip-layer guidance (SLG) skips a block instead and keeps chunk 2+ smooth!

Can we focus this smoothness on moving regions?

We predict patch-wise teacher uncertainty and apply stronger soft-skip guidance where uncertainty is high (moving patches).

This smooths high-motion regions further at zero extra inference cost (still 4 NFE).

"Anti-drifting guidance also trains on student rollouts. Doesn't it hurt diversity like DMD?"

No, both quality and diversity improve!

DMD optimizes reverse-KL on clean x_0, while anti-drifting only tilts velocities at noisy x_t, keeping noise-to-output coupling.

Takeaways:

- Few-step AR video doesn't need DMD or a 14B bidirectional teacher.
- In AR FlowMap, teacher guidance is key:
1. Guiding away from student drift fixes exposure bias while keeping diversity.
2. Guiding smoothness on uncertain patches fixes fast motion.
