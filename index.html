Can we distill few-step AR video models without DMD?

Yes! We analyze why AR FlowMap distillation fails and fix it, matching 14B DMD models using only a 1.3B AR teacher while preserving diversity.

Project page: https://enkeejunior1.github.io/ar-video-flowmap/

We found that FlowMap fails in two ways:

1. Drifting: errors accumulate across chunks over long rollouts
2. Low-quality motion: fast-moving regions turn blurry and distorted

Why do these two failures happen?

Failure mode 1: Drifting.

In AR FlowMap, teacher and student share the same history.

Under self-forcing, the AR teacher is conditioned on the student's already-drifted history. Trained only on clean video, the teacher fails to fix the error and continues the drift.

How do we fix this? The method is simple.

We train a drift model online on student rollouts to imitate its drifted transitions, and use it as the negative branch of guidance.

Subtracting the drift direction pushes the teacher back toward clean video!

Failure mode 2: Low-quality motion.

Why does the student fail to learn motion?

We found that patches with larger motion have a much less smooth teacher velocity field (higher Lipschitz), making fast motion hard to learn in a few steps.

And this velocity smoothness is closely tied to guidance.

In the first chunk, CFG smooths the velocity field, but in later chunks this effect largely disappears.

Surprisingly, skip-layer guidance (SLG), skipping a block on the negative branch, keeps later chunks smooth!

Our motion-aware guidance applies SLG more strongly on motion patches.

We predict patch-wise teacher uncertainty and apply stronger soft-skip guidance where uncertainty is high (moving patches).

This smooths fast-motion regions further, helping the student learn motion!

"Anti-drifting guidance is also reverse-KL. Doesn't it hurt diversity like DMD?"

No, surprisingly, it helps both quality and diversity!

DMD optimizes reverse-KL on clean x_0, while anti-drifting only tilts velocity at noisy x_t, largely keeping the teacher's trajectory.
